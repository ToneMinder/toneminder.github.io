Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 30, 2026
Last Updated: July 30, 2026
This policy explains what information the ToneMinder Android app (published by Cleggy Grove) accesses, how it is used, and how you stay in control.

Why ToneMinder Asks for Contacts Access

ToneMinder needs permission to access your contacts so it can:

  • Display the contacts stored on your phone
  • Show the ringtone currently assigned to each contact
  • Assign a selected or generated ringtone to a contact

Your Contact Information Stays on Your Phone

ToneMinder does not upload, transmit, share, sell, or send your contact information anywhere outside your phone.

Your contacts are not sent to ToneMinder, Cleggy Grove, advertisers, analytics services, cloud storage, or any other third party. ToneMinder does not use ads, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not require an account.

ToneMinder accesses only the contact information needed to display your contacts and manage ringtone assignments directly on your device. It does not modify names, phone numbers, email addresses, or any other contact information.

Your Ringtones and Settings Stay on Your Phone

When you generate and assign a ToneMinder ringtone, it is saved on your phone as an Android ringtone and assigned directly to the selected contact. The ringtone continues to work when ToneMinder is closed.

Ringtones that have already been generated, saved, and assigned also remain on your phone if you uninstall ToneMinder. Their contact assignments continue to work unless you later remove the ringtone, change the contact's ringtone, or delete the contact.

ToneMinder also stores small application preferences on your device, such as ringtone generation settings. This information is never transmitted off your phone.

Internet Use

Viewing your contacts and generating or assigning ringtones all happen entirely on your device and do not require an internet connection. The only time ToneMinder uses your internet connection is if you choose to tap a support or website link inside the app, which opens your browser or email app.

You Remain in Control

ToneMinder does not change a contact's ringtone until you tap Assign. You can change a contact back to the phone's default ringtone at any time by selecting Default Ringtone.

You can review or revoke ToneMinder's contacts permission at any time from your device's Settings → Apps → ToneMinder → Permissions. If you revoke this permission, ToneMinder will not be able to display your contacts or assign ringtones until the permission is granted again.

Children's Privacy

ToneMinder is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new Last Updated date above.
